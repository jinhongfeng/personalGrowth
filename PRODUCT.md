# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Personal Windows desktop users who want a local, low-friction place to keep growth records, account information, and recovery actions. They are often using the app during ordinary daily review moments, quick record entry, password lookup, or low-energy states where the interface needs to reduce effort rather than demand discipline.

## Product Purpose

Personal Growth Assistant helps users maintain order, growth, and a sense of agency. It combines three jobs: record personal progress, manage sensitive account details locally, and suggest small recovery actions when the user feels stuck, numb, or low on energy. Success means the user can add a record quickly, find important account information safely, and take a doable next action without feeling judged or overwhelmed.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, humane. The product should feel like a steady desktop companion: clear enough for repeated use, warm enough for personal reflection, and restrained enough to be trusted with sensitive information.

## Anti-references

Do not make it feel like a marketing landing page, a gamified habit app that pressures the user, or a decorative dashboard full of ornamental cards. Avoid loud gradients, over-designed controls, vague motivational copy, and password-manager UI that feels casual or insecure.

## Design Principles

1. Keep recording light: common entries should be reachable in a few clicks with templates and sensible defaults.
2. Make feedback visible: statistics and recent activity should show whether the user is moving forward.
3. Protect focus and trust: password and settings screens should use familiar, predictable affordances.
4. Lower the threshold in low-energy moments: recovery actions should be specific, small, and easy to complete.
5. Keep modules connected: the dashboard should explain why growth, security, and recovery belong in one personal system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable, keyboard-friendly product UI with sufficient text contrast, visible focus states, and responsive layouts for laptop and narrow desktop windows. Motion should be short, state-driven, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences. Copy should be plain Chinese for primary UI labels, with concise button text that states the action.
